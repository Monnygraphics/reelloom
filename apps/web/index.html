What it does

One-click clean-up

Cuts silences, ums and uhs, repeated words and phrases, and retaken lines, then rebuilds the captions from what is left.

Designed captions

Word-by-word captions with highlight colours and pill backgrounds, in open-licence fonts.

Punch-in zoom

A subtle zoom on every other caption keeps the frame moving.

Even loudness

Export is levelled to -14 LUFS, the level Instagram and TikTok play at.

Vertical export

9:16 MP4 up to 1080p, encoded by your own graphics hardware.

Free to use

No account and no watermark. MIT licensed.

Early beta. Works today: multi-clip editing with undo, clean-up, on-device captions in 7 styles, transitions, punch-in zoom, split explainer pages, a Shorts finder, one-click Autopilot, loudness and MP4 export. An optional AI helper uses your own free key.

Your video stays yours

The editor runs inside your browser. Editing, captions and export all happen on your device, so nothing is uploaded and there is no server holding your footage.

  • No upload of video or audio
  • No API key in the page
  • AI is optional: on-device first, your own key second
  • Only open-licence fonts and assets
