What it does

Auto-edit

Finds retakes and long pauses and cuts them, so you start from a tight take.

Designed captions

Word-by-word captions with highlight colours and pill backgrounds, in open-licence fonts.

Punch-ins and pages

Subtle zooms and split explainer pages that follow what you say.

Shorts finder

Point it at a long recording and it suggests the clips worth posting.

Vertical export

9:16 MP4 up to 1080p, encoded by your own graphics hardware.

Free to use

No account and no watermark. MIT licensed.

Your video stays yours

The editor runs inside your browser. Editing, captions and export all happen on your device, so nothing is uploaded and there is no server holding your footage.

  • No upload of video or audio
  • No API key in the page
  • AI is optional: on-device first, your own key second
  • Only open-licence fonts and assets
